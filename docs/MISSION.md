# Mission: Saudi Qudrat Exam — Quantitative Section (القسم الكمي)

## Why
The user is a recent high school graduate applying to a competitive engineering program that requires a strong Qudrat score. They need to score above 95% on the quantitative section, with the exam scheduled within about one month.

## Success looks like
- Scores 95%+ on official/high-fidelity Qudrat quantitative practice tests under real timing, consistently (not a one-off lucky run)
- Solves quantitative comparison (المقارنات) questions using estimation/elimination technique instead of full calculation, in under ~20-25 seconds each
- Correctly and quickly handles the four major content areas: arithmetic & ratios, algebra & equations (~23% of questions), geometry (~24%), and statistics/probability (~13%)
- Reads and solves word problems and data-table/chart questions in Arabic at exam speed, without needing a calculator
- Has run at least one full-length timed practice section in the final week and reviewed every miss

## Constraints
- ~1 month until the exam — intensive pace required (user has 9+ hours/week available)
- Study and practice must be in **Arabic** to match the real exam's language and build reading speed under time pressure
- User has not yet taken a diagnostic — we start teaching immediately and calibrate difficulty as we go, using early lessons to infer their actual level
- Recent high school graduate — core math content (algebra, geometry, arithmetic) should be fresh, so the biggest early leverage is likely in Qudrat-specific question formats and speed technique, not re-teaching math from scratch. Confirm/revise this assumption as evidence comes in.

## Out of scope
- The verbal section (القسم اللفظي) — this workspace is quantitative-only
- Deep theoretical math beyond what Qudrat actually tests (e.g., calculus, advanced proofs) — Qudrat quantitative stays at a high-school level, so no need to go beyond it
