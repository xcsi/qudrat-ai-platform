# Qudrat Quantitative Section Resources

## Knowledge

- [هيئة تقويم التعليم والتدريب (ETEC) — مركز قياس](https://etec.gov.sa/ar/centers/qiyas/p/p-7d1870ff-a881-4e9a-9f9d-8f2a2c4df032) / [qiyas.sa](https://qiyas.sa/)
  The official Saudi assessment body that writes and administers the real Qudrat exam. This is the single highest-trust source for exam structure, official sample questions, and registration. Use for: confirming exam format changes, official practice tests, and anything where "is this actually how the real exam works" matters. **Action for user**: log into your own Qiyas/Noor account to pull the official trial test (النموذج التجريبي) — access is account-gated so I can't fetch it directly.
  Note on content weighting (from published breakdowns, cross-check against the official sample test as we go): algebra & equations ≈ 23% of questions, geometry ≈ 24%, statistics & probability ≈ 13%, with the remainder split across arithmetic/ratios and quantitative comparisons (المقارنات الكمية).
